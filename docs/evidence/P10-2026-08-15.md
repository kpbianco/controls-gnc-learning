## Positive, negative, recovery, isolation, compatibility, and bounds

- Positive: deterministic repeated baselines, exact coefficient sum, split-command
  recurrence, shifted previous-command state, exact on-grid and non-commensurate
  sample/switch events, continuous target reference points, both isolated sweeps,
  state-matrix and pole residuals, display/sample reconstruction, and learner CLI
  selection.
- Negative and malformed input: NaN/Inf, negative delay, nonscalar display step,
  `Td>Ts`, excessive sample period/horizon, under-resolved display, excessive grids,
  event-enriched display-grid overflow, and divergent response are rejected or bounded.
- Limiting cases: `Td=0` gives zero stale-command weight; `Td=Ts` gives zero new-
  command weight and one full old-command interval; small zero-delay `Ts` approaches
  the continuous target; a horizon shorter than `Td` retains the initial command;
  a noninteger endpoint preserves the exact final partial-delay motion.
- Failure and recovery: `Ts=0.2 s`, `Td=0.18 s` has Nyquist ratio above 1.7 but
  spectral radius above 1.13 and growing deviation; reducing only `Td` to `0.02 s`
  gives spectral radius below 0.37 and converges while preserving `Ts`, gain, and plant.
- Isolation: `model.m` has no plotting, randomness, filesystem, network, timer,
  pause, global, persistent, or asynchronous path. P10 tests own permanent P10 facts
  and allow future batches to advance the shared frontier.
- Compatibility: only base MATLAB arithmetic plus established `arguments`, `uifigure`,
  `uigridlayout`, `uiaxes`, and `uislider` APIs are used. Licensed MATLAB release,
  Octave, Windows/PowerShell, and rendered UI compatibility remain unverified.
- Resource bounds: horizon is capped at `30 s`, `Ts` at `0.5 s`, controller samples at
  10,001, display samples at 30,001, and response/command magnitude at 1,000. Exact cap
  cases are retained as accepted tests; tiny intervals are rejected before allocation,
  and the display cap is rechecked after exact timing events are merged into the grid.
- Timeout and cancellation are not applicable: the model is bounded synchronous
  arithmetic with no worker, external service, persistent transaction, or background
  task. Interactive callbacks perform one bounded redraw and own no external state.
- Rollback/recovery is file-based and non-destructive; no migration, database,
  persisted schema, scoring state, package, service, or external data changed.
- Security/licensing surface is unchanged: P10 adds no network, process, filesystem,
  credential, deserialization, third-party dependency, or copied asset path.

## Final audit

The system-risk audits found and repaired three in-scope issues:

1. `run_checks.m` originally proved the command identity at the delay boundary and a
   pre-switch partial interval, but did not independently prove output propagation
   after the switch. It now checks the exact boundary output and the first
   post-switch exponential point; the focused static test retains that check marker.
2. The evidence inventory originally described the broken timing pair as outside the
   interactive range. The sliders actually allow `Ts=0.2 s`, `Td/Ts=0.9`; the inventory
   now correctly states that the scripted failure is also interactively reachable.
3. Controller samples, computation-delay switches, and display points were generated
   on independent floating-point grids. A mathematically coincident event such as the
   baseline sample near `0.15 s` could therefore be classified after the regular display
   point and appear one display step late in the command trace. `model.m` now merges
   exact sample and switch instants into the display grid, prefers event timestamps over
   rounding-near regular points, and reapplies the 30,001-point cap after the merge.
   `run_checks.m` and the focused Python suite now retain a non-commensurate
   `Ts=0.033 s`, `Td=0.011 s`, `displayStep=0.01 s` behavioral regression covering the
   first switch, next sample, second switch, command identities, output continuity,
   and the post-merge display-resource cap.
