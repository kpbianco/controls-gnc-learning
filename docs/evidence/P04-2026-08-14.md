  initial states, exact initial accelerations, exact linear quarter-cycle propagation
  from nonzero rate, P03 characteristic roots, period, restoring magnitude, zero-cross
  delay, damping energy direction, both isolated levers, sign symmetry, and
  accepted-resolution timing/energy.
- Limiting cases: microscopic release, exact zero equilibrium, tiny-angle model
  agreement, undamped linear quadratic-energy conservation, and undamped nonlinear
  physical-energy conservation.
- Negative/malformed cases: nonfinite, nonreal, nonscalar, negative damping,
  nonpositive duration/interval, upright/out-of-range angle, excessive rate,
  length/damping range, under-resolved grid, and excessive sample grid are rejected.

  first-zero delay and more than 60 degrees maximum disagreement; five degrees

  final interval, maximum 20,001 samples, larger-grid rejection, and `wn*h <= 0.075`.
- Isolation: the model is synchronous and presentation-free with no file, network,
  process, service, random, timer, global, or persistent state; CLI tests use
  temporary repository fixtures.
- Compatibility: only base MATLAB operations and the `arguments`, `uifigure`, and
