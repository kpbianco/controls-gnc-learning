## Governing model and independent reference results

P18 retains P17's damped cart and exact baseline gain at the `0.02 s` design interval. The gain is
declared directly and held fixed when the display interval changes; P18 does not rerun or imply an LQR
optimization. The reference is generated by a feasible held plant-input acceleration, not by asserting
a sinusoidal position:

```text
x_ref[k+1] = A*x_ref[k] + B*u_plan[k]
e[k]       = x_ref[k] - x[k]
u_ff[k]    = s_ff*alpha*u_plan[k]
u_fb[k]    = beta*K*e[k]
u_cmd[k]   = u_ff[k] + u_fb[k]
x[k+1]     = A*x[k] + B*(u_cmd[k] + d[k])
e[k+1]     = (A-beta*B*K)*e[k] + B*((1-s_ff*alpha)*u_plan[k]-d[k])
```

For `alpha=1`, `beta=1`, correct polarity, `u_plan=0.6*sin(0.6*t) m/s^2`, a
`-0.4 m/s^2` load from `4 s` through `5 s`, `12 s` duration, and `dt=0.02 s`, the independent
Python oracle produced:

- `Ad(2,2)=0.9900498337491681`, `Ad(1,2)=0.019900332501663787 s`, and
  `Bd=[0.00019933499667242678; 0.019900332501663787]` in acceleration-input units;
- retained `K=[1.9644942427723904, 1.7703588392280174]` with units `1/s^2` and `1/s`;
- position-error integral `0.0161739933608727 m^2 s`, rate-error integral
  `0.01669464795376342 m^2/s`, and position RMSE `0.036682286326667535 m`;
- feedforward, feedback, and total-command squared integrals of
  `2.0128906150791637`, `0.11474509167732859`, and `2.246753457456084 m^2/s^3`;
- maximum position error `0.11172189006637057 m`, maximum feedback correction
  `0.38836234256894503 m/s^2`, and `2.6 s` error-recovery time after load removal.

With the disturbance removed, the `alpha=1` limit has exactly zero state error and feedback command.
The `alpha=[0,0.5,1,1.5]` position-error integrals are symmetric about one, with the middle matched
case exactly zero. With matched feedforward and the load restored, increasing
`beta=[0,0.25,0.5,1,1.5,2]` decreases position-error integral from `2.922270530744645` to
`0.004266822197263013 m^2 s` and increases feedback-correction integral from zero to
`0.14107214810495575 m^2/s^3`. Feedforward alone retains a unit error pole and ends with
`0.7809892087462634 m` position error after the pulse. Reversed feedforward has
`1.8206989696234246 m^2 s` position-error integral, `9.143306969415665 m^2/s^3` feedback-correction
integral, and no recovery by the fixed horizon; a fresh correct-sign call exactly equals baseline.

Component squared integrals are intentionally reported separately. They are not added as energies:
the squared total command contains the feedforward-feedback cross term.
