## Governing model and independent reference results

P17 retains P16's damped cart, uses an exact zero-order hold, and makes the discrete LQR operation
visible:

```text
p_dot = v
v_dot = -0.5*v + u
J = integral(q_p*(p/1 m)^2 + (v/(1 m/s))^2 + r*(u/(1 m/s^2))^2) dt
P = Q + A'*P*A - A'*P*B*(R+B'*P*B)^(-1)*B'*P*A
K = (R+B'*P*B)^(-1)*B'*P*A
u[k] = -K*x[k]
x[k+1] = A*x[k] + effectiveness*B*u[k]
```

The normalized integrand is dimensionless; its time integral has seconds. The physical squared-
acceleration effort integral has `m^2/s^3` and is not called mechanical or electrical energy.
Position and rate gain components are reported separately as `1/s^2` and `1/s`. No LQR, Riccati,
state-space, discretization, simulation, inverse, or pseudoinverse helper hides the operation.

For `q_p=4`, `r=1`, effectiveness `1`, initial position `1 m`, duration `12 s`, and `dt=0.02 s`,
independent Python arithmetic produced:

- `Ad(2,2)=0.9900498337491681`, `Ad(1,2)=0.019900332501663787 s`, and
  `Bd=[0.00019933499667242678; 0.019900332501663787]` in acceleration-input units;
- Riccati convergence in 563 iterations and `K=[1.9644942427742105 1.7703588392290937]`;
- poles `0.9772137558739515 ± 0.01620706176962395i`, strictly inside the unit circle;
- settling time `3.34 s`, position ISE `0.8011009435612685 m^2 s`, and squared-command effort
  integral `0.9818849175086549 m^2/s^3`;
- running score `4.622785426936517 s`, terminal score `7.241575355485134e-12 s`, and initial
  Riccati value `4.6227854268384325 s`, agreeing within the retained iteration tolerance;
- final state `[-1.6678972128381668e-06 m; 1.2948626180860713e-06 m/s]`.

The exact `q_p=0` limit has zero position gain and input, persistent `1 m` position, a unit pole,
and no settlement. The broken effectiveness-zero case retains nominal `P`, `K`, and poles but has
actual poles approximately `1` and `0.9900498337491692`, zero applied acceleration, persistent
position, and `46.31085155871597 m^2/s^3` requested-effort integral. The slowest accepted checked
Riccati case (`q_p=0.25`, `r=20`, `dt=0.0016 s`) converged in 30,947 of 40,000 bounded iterations on
the maximum 5,000-step grid.
