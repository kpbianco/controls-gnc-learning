## Governing model and independent reference results

P16 retains P15's damped cart with `d = 0.5 1/s`, known acceleration command `u`, uncertain
acceleration `w`, and two independent position measurements:

```text
p_dot = v
v_dot = -d*v + u + w
y = [p + n_A + outlier; p + n_B]
xminus[k] = Ad*xplus[k-1] + Bd*u[k-1]
Pminus[k] = Ad*Pplus[k-1]*Ad' + Q
innovation[k] = y[k] - C*xminus[k]
S[k] = C*Pminus[k]*C' + R
K[k] = Pminus[k]*C'*S[k]^(-1)
xplus[k] = xminus[k] + K[k]*innovation[k]
```

`Q = Bd*Bd'*sigma_a^2`, `R = diag([sigma_A^2, sigma_B^2])`, and the posterior covariance uses
the Joseph form. The two-by-two `S` inverse is expanded from its determinant. No Kalman, Riccati,
state-space, simulation, random-stream, or matrix-inverse helper hides the operation.

For seed `1601`, `dt = 0.05 s`, duration `20 s`, assumed/actual acceleration standard deviation
`0.08 m/s^2`, sensor A standard deviation `0.35 m`, and sensor B standard deviation `0.8 m`,
independent Python arithmetic produced:

- `Ad(2,2) = 0.9753099120283326` and `Ad(1,2) = 0.04938017594333477 s`;
- `Bd = [0.001239648113330466; 0.04938017594333477]` in acceleration-input units;
- `Q(1,1) = 9.835055647256216e-09 m^2` and `R = diag([0.1225, 0.64]) m^2`;
- first process pseudo-noise values `-2.4787634175430178`, `-1.6170925134602487`;
- last-five-second position RMSE `0.07875913925071903 m` and rate RMSE
  `0.009304162796690399 m/s`;
- mean last-five-second NIS `1.772969285600885` for two measurement components;
- steady sensor-A/sensor-B position gain entries `0.015196753887173783` and
  `0.002908753673716856`;
- steady rate gain from sensor A `0.002776780744930683 1/s`;
- posterior position/rate standard deviations `0.04314629012069042 m` and
  `0.01721870938103643 m/s`;
- outlier-sample NIS `98.72200161485344` versus clean `5.9247759358628`;
- largest accepted grid `5,000` steps and `5,001` finite samples.

The independent tests reconstruct every truth, state-prediction, covariance-prediction, innovation,
gain, state-update, Joseph-covariance, and NIS recurrence. They also verify covariance symmetry and
positive determinant, lower fused-position tail RMSE than either raw sensor on this fixed baseline,
equivalence between the simultaneous two-sensor update and independent scalar updates in either order,
isolated monotone gain/covariance sweep effects, data-independent covariance, single-sample outlier
isolation, exact fresh-call recovery, alternate-seed isolation, equal-reported-noise gain symmetry,
and the maximum resource grid.
