## Governing model and independent reference results

The first-order speed plant uses a sampled controller and exact zero-order-held plant propagation:

```text
u[k]   = (a0/b0)*r[k] + K*(r[k]-v[k])
v[k+1] = exp(-a*dt)*v[k] + (b/a)*(1-exp(-a*dt))*u[k]
v_ss   = b*(a0/b0+K)*r / (a+b*K)
```

Here `a0=1 1/s`, `b0=1` dimensionless, `K=1.5 1/s`, `dt=0.02 s`, the reference
steps from `0` to `1 m/s` at `1 s`, and the horizon is `10 s`. Nominal prediction and actual
plant close separate deterministic feedback loops so the retained difference is closed-loop
sensitivity to plant-model error.

The independent Python oracle produced these reference facts:

- baseline nominal decay `0.9801986733067553`, input coefficient
  `0.019801326693244747 s`, and sampled closed-loop pole `0.9504966832668882`;
- baseline predicted and actual histories exactly equal, with zero prediction-gap integral, RMSE,
  and peak gap;
- baseline steady speed `1 m/s`, actuator-gain local sensitivity
  `+0.4 m/s per fraction`, and drag local sensitivity `-0.4 m/s per fraction`;
- at actuator ratio `0.8`, prediction-gap integral `0.07788234685631548 m^2/s`, RMSE
  `0.08825636091170849 m/s`, peak gap `0.11304306795345398 m/s`, steady speed `10/11 m/s`,
  actuator sensitivity `0.5165289256198347 m/s per fraction`, and drag sensitivity
  `-0.4132231404958677 m/s per fraction`;
- the actuator sweep `[0.6,0.8,1,1.2,1.4]` increases steady speed monotonically from
  `15/19` to `35/31 m/s`, with a unique zero raw gap at ratio one;
- the drag sweep `[0.5,0.75,1,1.5,2]` decreases steady speed monotonically from `1.25` to
  `5/7 m/s`, with a unique zero raw gap at ratio one;
- reversed unit actuator polarity has pole `1.0099006633466223`, peak prediction gap
  `417.08121075943916 m/s`, and feedback squared-command integral
  `386102.2266402103 m^2/s^3`; its diverging trajectory has no defined steady-response sensitivity;
- a reversed-sign case with gain ratio `0.5` and drag ratio `2` remains dynamically stable with
  steady speed `-1 m/s`, but its sensitivities are intentionally undefined because polarity is
  outside the positive-ratio uncertainty family;
- zero reference from rest is an exact equilibrium, a fresh correct-sign call exactly recovers the
  baseline, and the maximum accepted `5,001`-sample positive-polarity run remains finite and stable.

The local sensitivity is the derivative of steady speed with respect to a parameter ratio, not the
finite secant from the nominal point. Raw prediction-gap RMSE and local sensitivity therefore occupy
separate plots and retain their distinct units.

### Metrics and units

- feedforward, feedback, and total commands: metres per second squared;
- prediction-gap and tracking-error squared integrals: square metres per second;
- feedback squared-command integral: square metres per cubic second;
- drag and feedback gain: inverse seconds;
- held-input coefficient: seconds;
- time, duration, and sample interval: seconds;
- actuator effectiveness, actuator/drag ratios, sign, pole, step/sample counts: dimensionless;
- local actuator-gain and drag sensitivities: metres per second per unit fractional ratio.
