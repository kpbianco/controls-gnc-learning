### Controls

- Reference `r`: slider from `-2` through `2 output`, baseline `1 output`.
- Actuator limit `uLimit`: slider from `0.25` through `3 actuator`, baseline
  `2 actuator`.
- Reset restores both baseline values.
- The deliberately broken `r=1.5`, `uLimit=0.6` pair and the same-reference
  `uLimit=2` recovery are reachable inside the bounded interactive ranges.

### Metrics and units

- Requested/applied control, clipping gap, saturation mask, saturation duration and
  fraction, first-clipping and release times, asymptotic-persistence and
  finite-horizon/end saturation flags, peak
  requested/applied command, peak clipping gap, limited-versus-unlimited output gap,
  final tracking error, integral absolute error, actuator-authority indicators,
  P-only equilibrium, time/grid settings, and sample/resource counts.
- Time, horizon, interval, saturation duration, and release time: seconds (`s`).
- Output, reference, tracking error, and limited/unlimited gap: normalized `output`.
- Requested/applied command, clipping gap, and limit: normalized `actuator`.
- Integral absolute error: `output*s`.
- Controller gain: `actuator/output`; plant static gain: `output/actuator`.
- Authority ratio and saturation fraction/percent: dimensionless.

## Positive, negative, recovery, isolation, compatibility, and bounds

- Positive: deterministic repeated baselines, explicit symmetric clamp, requested
  proportional command, exact held-input recurrence, baseline release at `0.29 s`,
  unlimited equivalence under high authority, both isolated sweep trends, and CLI start.
- Negative and malformed input: NaN/Inf, nonscalar reference, zero/negative/excessive
  limit, excessive reference/horizon/time step, and excessive sample grids are
  rejected before unsafe allocation where applicable.
- Limiting cases: zero reference stays exactly zero; a high limit exactly recovers the
  unlimited path; positive and negative steps are sign-symmetric; persistent clipping
  reduces to `y=uLimit*(1-exp(-t))`; a noninteger horizon keeps its exact final interval.
- Failure and recovery: `r=1.5`, `uLimit=0.6` remains clipped for the full view and
  retains more than `0.9 output` final error; increasing only `uLimit` to `2` releases
  clipping and recovers toward the unchanged `1.2 output` P-only equilibrium.
- Isolation: `model.m` has no plotting, randomness, filesystem, network, process,
  timer, pause, global, persistent, or asynchronous path. P11 tests own permanent P11
  facts and allow later batches to advance the shared frontier.
- Compatibility: only base MATLAB arithmetic plus established `arguments`,
  `uifigure`, `uigridlayout`, `uiaxes`, and `uislider` APIs are used. Licensed MATLAB
  release, Octave, Windows/PowerShell, and rendered UI compatibility remain unverified.
- Resource bounds: reference magnitude is capped at `5 output`, actuator limit at
  `10 actuator`, horizon at `30 s`, time step at `0.05 s`, response magnitude at 100,
  and samples at 30,001. The exact maximum sample grid is accepted; a tiny interval is
  rejected before allocation.
- Timeout and cancellation are not applicable: the model is bounded synchronous
  arithmetic with no worker, external service, persistent transaction, or background
  task. Interactive callbacks perform one bounded redraw and own no external state.
- Rollback/recovery is file-based and non-destructive; no migration, database,
  persisted schema, scoring state, package, service, or external data changed.
- Security/licensing surface is unchanged: P11 adds no network, process, filesystem,
  credential, deserialization, third-party dependency, or copied asset path.

## Final system-risk audit and repairs

Bounded system-risk audits found and drove these in-scope repairs:

1. A valid horizon smaller than the grid tolerance could replace the initial `t=0`
   sample rather than append the positive endpoint. `model.m` now special-cases a
   zero regular-interval count, and MATLAB/Python checks retain `t=[0;1e-16]`.
2. Full-view clipping was originally labeled persistent even when an actuator limit
   just above the required equilibrium command would release after the five-second
   view. The model now derives `persistentSaturation` from equilibrium authority and
   reports `saturatedThroughHorizon` separately. Checks cover exact-horizon release
   and clipped-through-view/eventual-release cases.
3. The first draft used distinct output/actuator labels without declaring the
   conversion gains. The governing equation now explicitly declares `tau=1 s`,
   plant gain `g=1 output/actuator`, and `Kp=4 actuator/output`; the model retains
   plant gain and required equilibrium command as named metrics.
4. Requested, applied, and missing held commands originally used linearly interpolated
   plots. Lesson, experiment, and interactive command views now use `stairs` so
   presentation matches the held-input mechanism.
5. The retained evidence file was created before final completion and updated after
   repairs with the latest full-verification log and explicit claim boundaries.
6. The P11-specific suite did not behaviorally cover the newly enabled public learner
   `check` route. It now executes `./bin/learn check P11` and requires the exact
   `run_module_checks('P11')` dispatch instruction without claiming MATLAB execution.

After repairs, focused P11, contract, quick, full, learner-CLI, JSON, whitespace,
EOF, allowed-path, and durable-frontier audits passed. No remaining material static
finding was accepted as resolved by MATLAB execution; that runtime remains unavailable.
